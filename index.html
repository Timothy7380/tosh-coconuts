Big goodness inside every coconut

One fruit, a whole kitchen. We turn fresh coconuts into drinks, oils and flours that fit into everyday Nigerian cooking.

Naturally hydrating

Coconut water is naturally rich in electrolytes like potassium. It's a refreshing drink on hot Lagos afternoons.

Dairy-free creaminess

Our full-cream coconut milk works for smoothies, rice, stews and baking.

Gluten-free swaps

Coconut flour, poundo and garri are naturally gluten-free, with the familiar texture you already love.

Cold-pressed oil

Pressed without high heat, so it keeps its fresh coconut aroma. Use it for cooking, skin and hair.
